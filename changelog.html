What's changed, build by build. Newest first.

Ver P7XK15 Nov 2026
  • New Admin "Activity log" panel — shows recent person edits and membership changes (who, what, when), pulled from the change history the database already keeps automatically. Admins and moderators only.
  • Added this changelog page, linked from the Admin page's version line.
  • "Discard Current Changes" (under Rearrange) now only shows up when there's something from the current session to discard.
Ver N4QZ15 Nov 2026
  • "Ref" renamed to "Ver" throughout.
  • A small "vXXXX" watermark now floats in the bottom-right corner of the tree canvas at all times.
  • The Admin page now shows the current version and when it was last updated.
Ver D6HP15 Nov 2026
  • Rearrange is now its own expandable section in the menu (like Custom Views), with three options: Rearrange Tiles / Exit Rearrange, Discard Current Changes, and Reset All Rearrangements.
  • Starting Rearrange Tiles closes the menu for a clear canvas; exiting commits what you moved.
Ver W9BL15 Nov 2026
  • Added a Reset control next to Rearrange, to clear every manual card position at once.
Ver K2FY15 Nov 2026
  • The collapse arrow for a child/sibling now sits above their card (toward the parent) instead of below.
  • Confirmed the spouse collapse arrow is gold/brown, pointing toward the partner when visible and outward once collapsed.
Ver R5TQ15 Nov 2026
  • New "Rearrange" mode: drag any card to move it and everyone below them — spouse, children, grandchildren — as one group. The connecting lines follow automatically.
  • Manual positions persist for the session and can be saved into a Custom View.
Ver J8WN15 Nov 2026
  • Widened the gap between spouse cards so the collapse arrow has proper room, and fixed it losing its gold outline when squeezed.
  • Collapsing a person's only spouse no longer hides their shared children — only her card disappears; multi-spouse collapsing (each wife's own children fold with her) was already correct and is unchanged.
Ver V4RM15 Nov 2026
  • Redesigned the Collapse control end to end: no more "+Name" pills — a single unlabeled gold arrow per relationship now lives on the anchor card in both states, flipping direction to collapse or restore.
  • Added an eye-icon toggle next to the hamburger menu to hide/show every collapse arrow at once, for a clean view.
Earlier builds
Ver P3WY
  • Restyled the first version of the Collapse control to reuse the existing triangle-arrow look.
Ver X6RT
  • Added the permanent "Hidden People" feature — hide anyone from your view until you explicitly unhide them, from its own section in the menu.
Ver Q9LZ
  • First version of on-canvas Collapse, generalised to every relationship (not just spouses), downward and sideways.
Ver H4PV
  • Session-only collapse/expand, and persisted Custom Views (save, load, delete, up to 5 named views).

Builds before this point aren't listed here.
